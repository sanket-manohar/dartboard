# See the big picture

Click the **graph** button in the panel (or the Total row) to open **Insights**:

- **Coverage map:** every file as a box, sized by lines of code and coloured by coverage. Weak areas jump out. Click a box to open the file.
- **History:** your total coverage after every run in this workspace, with the commit that caused the biggest drop.
- **Risk radar:** the files most likely to hide bugs, ranked by low coverage, complexity and (with git) how often they change.

Everything is computed locally. No account or internet needed.
