# Share an audit report

Click the **PDF** icon at the top of the panel. Dartboard writes `coverage/dartboard-report.pdf` with:

- an overall grade and the key numbers (lines, branches, screens, states, passing tests)
- what to do next, in order
- coverage by folder and by app layer, the riskiest files and files without tests
- screens and state classes your tests never reached
- failures, the slowest tests, screen size results and test mistakes

It's built locally from your last run. Nothing leaves your machine.
